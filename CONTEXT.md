# CONTEXT.md glossary

Client: Kenyon College Varsity Baseball Team Alumni.

**Head Coach (Matt Burdette)**: The site's owner. Holds the *admin* role and can edit everything, including the Team Schedule.

**Second Editor (Joe)**: Holds the *editor* role. Can write and publish any content, including Alumni Events and the Alumni Network, except Locked pages such as the Team Schedule. Cannot manage users.

**Team Schedule**: The current team's schedule, a single page in the public navigation. Only the Head Coach can edit it.

**Alumni Events**: The public section for alumni gatherings. It is a page in the navigation that lists individual Event posts.

**Post Type**: The required choice on every post: *Event* or *Team Update*. It decides where the post appears.

**Event**: A post of type Event, such as a reunion or game-watch. Date and place go in the title and body. It appears on Alumni Events and the Home page.

**Team Update**: A post of type Team Update, with news about the current team for fans, parents, students and alumni. It appears on the Home page.

**Home**: The front page. A welcome text, the latest three Team Updates, the most recent Events, and links to Team Schedule, Alumni Events and Alumni Network. Both editors can change it.

**Deactivate**: Turn off a user's account so they cannot log in. Users are never deleted, so authors and "recorded by" always point at a real person. The Head Coach cannot deactivate or demote himself.

**Alumni Network**: The public section listing alumni who have chosen to be listed. Shows names only; emails are never published.

**Listing**: One alumnus's entry in the Alumni Network. Holds name, email, optional class year, consent date, and who recorded it. Only listings with consent reach the public site.

**Opt-in**: An alumnus agrees to be listed. Alumni cannot log in, so the Second Editor records the consent when the alumnus asks to be listed.

**Consent date**: When an alumnus asked to be listed. Stored on the Listing.

**Alumnus**: A former player on the varsity team. Never a user of the admin console.

**Locked**: A page only the Head Coach may change. The Team Schedule is Locked. The Second Editor can open a Locked page to read it, but cannot edit, publish, unpublish or delete it. Only the Head Coach can lock or unlock a page.

**Alumni Contact Line**: The single public line on the Alumni Network page telling visitors to email the Second Editor, who then introduces them to the alumnus. It is the only way to reach an alumnus through the site.

**Publish**: The explicit step that turns published content into the public site. Edits are not live until the Head Coach (or the builder, in the demo) runs it.
