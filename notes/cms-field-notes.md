# CMS field notes: WordPress Playground

1. The biggest surprise to me was how easy it is to create and publish the playground as a website. It is a few simple clicks and you can view the website.

2. The part that I struggled with was creating a page and viewing it. I didn't realize you needed to republish it once I created the about page. 

3. Something else that took me a little bit of playing around to figure out was that the blogs and pages are all apart of the same website. I at first thought they were separate but realized by clicking on my dashboard they are connected. 

4. It looks to me like an editor can change the titles and words, as well as add in photos. However, an editor is unable to manage any of the websites settings such as deleting it.

5. I also noticed it seemed very easy to add in images and change the layout of the posts. WordPress offers templates as well which could be very useful for making a consistent layout.
